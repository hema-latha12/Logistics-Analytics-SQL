## 4. Product Analysis

## 5. Warehouse Analysis
- Identified warehouses handling the highest number of orders.

## 6. Shipping Analysis

## 8. Key Takeaways
- Order status distribution provides insight into logistics performance.
- Customer spending analysis helps identify high-value customers.
- Product analysis helps understand product demand.
- Warehouse stock utilisation helps monitor inventory levels.
